## 1. Capacity — the axis that orders everything else

**45 hub models run on NeuroBrix on 4x V100 (2016-class silicon),
across nine families.** Distribution honesty (2026-09-01): **38 are
PUBLIC** — seven carry a DEPRECATED badge with a user-language reason
while their published defect is fixed (each returns to public with its
validated fix; the badge reaches the user before the download).
First climb-back: TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat republished 2026-09-01 with the
prompt-aware KV sizing fix (`8da28e9`). Against each competitor runtime,
the coverage question is asked NOMINATIVELY (each DNR is sourced and
dated in its segment prereg, never estimated):

| runtime | runs (of 45) | cannot start — the nominative list |
|---|---|---|
| **NeuroBrix** | **45** | — |
| ollama | 2-3 (GGUF text ports of our LLM/code rows) | EVERY image, video, upscaler, TTS, STT, audio-LLM, VLM, omni model (no serving for these modalities); Qwen3-MoE-30B class only via community quants (class-mismatch) |
| vLLM | 0 on this hardware | vLLM>=0.7 dropped V100 wheels; 0.7.3-source refuses Qwen3MoE on sm_70 (S1 sourced verdict); no video/image/TTS/upscaler pipelines at all |
| diffusers | 14 (image+video+upscaler diffusion classes) | every LLM, STT, TTS, audio-LLM, VLM; Open-Sora-v2 (no pipeline) |
| faster-whisper | 2 (whisper family) | everything else |
| NeMo | 1 (parakeet) | everything else |
| vendor transformers | ~20 (traced-from stacks, one venv per model class) | needs a bespoke environment per model; no unified serving |

The point is the CONJUNCTION: one engine, one container format, one
CLI serves all 45 — the competitor column is a UNION of six tools,
each covering its slice, none crossing families.

## 3. The named fronts (strategy twins, written not implied)

1. **Prefill/TTFT is the widest open front**: x4.0-8.0 behind ollama
   at three contexts (S1). Chantier OPEN (owner-validated) with first
   numbers: the admissible formulation is a HOUSE large-M Triton GEMM
   (tl.dot is legitimate at prefill M; the "no tl.dot" lesson holds
   only at M=1) through the existing mm/addmm wrapper, autotuned E2.
   Quiet-host probes (house wrapper vs cuBLAS, best-of-space): the
   large-M ratio is **8.6-10x at every LLM shape** — the sm_70
   tl.dot wall flown at the flag (an initial "54.7x hole at M=2048"
   was retracted as a loaded-machine artifact; the autotuner selects
   the floor of its space). On this card the pure-GEMM lever is
   bounded; the same kernel unlocks on Ampere/Hopper. strided_copy
   at 35.7% of the decode timeline is the parallel lever; the next
   step is the internal TTFT bar (compiled vs triton, three
   contexts) to split the gap into GEMM-bound vs launch/copy-bound. R33 dynamic provenance audit on nsys decode +
   prefill timelines: CLEAN — every kernel ours or Triton-runtime.
   Byte gate + oracle each step; the decode band closed the same way.
2. **The Triton-Volta conv/mm gap is structural and named** — it
   prices every triton diffusion/upscaler row (x3-9); it is NOT a
   defect of those rows. sm_70 HMMA is reachable only through
   cuBLAS/cuDNN today.
3. **The STT baseline above is the third "before" on record** (text
   x105 -> closed; conv gap named; STT 0.3-1.7x RTFx dated today).
