## 1. Capacity — the axis that orders everything else

**45 hub models run on NeuroBrix on 4x V100 (2016-class silicon),
across nine families.** Distribution honesty (2026-09-02 10:45 UTC):
**42 are PUBLIC** — three carry a DEPRECATED badge with a user-language
reason while their published defect is fixed (each returns to public
with its validated fix; the badge reaches the user before the
download). Climb-back so far: TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat (prompt-aware KV
sizing, `8da28e9`) and resemble-ai/Chatterbox (speech-head dtype,
`ca2e369`), republished 2026-09-01; nvidia/Parakeet-TDT-1.1B and
hpcai-tech/Open-Sora-v2 (long-form STT + NeMo-exact greedy; chunk
retry, `2f57c91`) and Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-Video-2B-720p
(container re-created by the fixed tracer, uploaded through the new
multipart path), all three republished 2026-09-02; canopylabs/Orpheus-3B
returned to public the same day once the per-step margin instrument
showed the triton engine following the PyTorch oracle 432/432 (the
audible difference was an exact fp16 tie on the compiled side, not a
defect). **43 PUBLIC.** Remaining badges, each with its precise reason:
Allegro-TI2V (its strided temporal slice is now symbolic at the source,
but its denoiser was traced at a degenerate batch of 1 by the synthetic
Phase A and the CFG batch folds into the token axis — a non-unit-batch
stimulus with every input batched is the named next step) and VACE (a
T=1 degenerate merge that symbolized a real channel width, an unrolled
chunk loop, a spatial conditioning mismatch and an error-700 triton
fault — the loop-aware tracer chantier).

## 3. The named fronts (strategy twins, written not implied)

1. **Prefill/TTFT is the widest open front**: x4.0-8.0 behind ollama
   at three contexts (S1). Chantier OPEN (owner-validated) with first
   numbers: the admissible formulation is a HOUSE large-M Triton GEMM
   (tl.dot is legitimate at prefill M; the "no tl.dot" lesson holds
   only at M=1) through the existing mm/addmm wrapper, autotuned E2.
   Quiet-host probes (house wrapper vs cuBLAS, best-of-space): the
   large-M ratio is **8.6-10x at every LLM shape** — the sm_70
   tl.dot wall flown at the flag (an initial "54.7x hole at M=2048"
   was retracted as a loaded-machine artifact; the autotuner selects
   the floor of its space). On this card the pure-GEMM lever is
   bounded; the same kernel unlocks on Ampere/Hopper. strided_copy
   at 35.7% of the decode timeline is the parallel lever; the next
   step is the internal TTFT bar (compiled vs triton, three
   contexts) to split the gap into GEMM-bound vs launch/copy-bound. R33 dynamic provenance audit on nsys decode +
   prefill timelines: CLEAN — every kernel ours or Triton-runtime.
   Byte gate + oracle each step; the decode band closed the same way.
   **09-02 — the nsys split of ONE xlong (~8.3k) triton prefill on the
   canonical int4 row** (validation_outputs/prefill_nsys_2026_09_02/
   SPLIT.md): 172 s total, 18.3 s of kernels over 5,811 launches —
   SDPA chunked math 34.8 % (baddbmm, the tl.dot wall), MoE 22.9 %,
   elementwise 15.4 % (fp32 MoE intermediates at ~650 GB/s: a fusion
   lever, the kernel itself runs at 848 GB/s), copies 14.1 %, dense GEMM
   6.5 % — and on the host **5,809 cudaMalloc + 5,773 cudaFree = 82 s**.
   **Lever 1 measured** (LEVER1_POOL_AB.md, interleaved n=3, locked
   clock, bytes identical): the in-house allocator pool
   (NBX_ALLOC_POOL=1) takes the cold xlong run from 152.5 s to 90.1 s
   median (**−41 %**), first token 161.8 s → 99.4 s (−39 %). Default-on
   is the next gated act (full-zoo byte gate + serve-warm smoke).
2. **The Triton-Volta conv/mm gap is structural and named** — it
   prices every triton diffusion/upscaler row (x3-9); it is NOT a
   defect of those rows. sm_70 HMMA is reachable only through
   cuBLAS/cuDNN today.
3. **The STT baseline above is the third "before" on record** (text
   x105 -> closed; conv gap named; STT 0.3-1.7x RTFx dated today).
