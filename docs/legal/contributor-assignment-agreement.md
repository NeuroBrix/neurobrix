# NeuroBrix Individual Contributor Assignment Agreement — version 1.0

(referred to as "the NeuroBrix Contributor Assignment Agreement")

Modelled on the Project Harmony Contributor Assignment Agreement v1.0 (https://www.harmonyagreements.org), used under
the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license, modified for the NeuroBrix projects. Thank you for your interest in contributing to the NeuroBrix projects. To keep the projects able
to evolve, be licensed and be maintained over time, the rights in every Contribution are held by one party: the Owner.
This Agreement transfers those rights to the Owner and gives You back a broad license to Your own Contribution.

## 1. Definitions

**"Owner"** means the owner of the NeuroBrix project: the holder of the rights in the Projects as identified in the
NOTICE file of the repository to which the Contribution is submitted, at the time of the Contribution, together with
its successors and assigns.

**"Projects"** means the software and documentation published under the NeuroBrix name by the Owner, including
neurobrix, nbxtensor, NBXGrammar, NBX Code, the NeuroBrix hub and any later NeuroBrix project, excluding forks of
third-party projects, which keep their own terms.

**"You"** means the individual who submits a Contribution and accepts this Agreement. A legal entity contributing
through its employees signs the Entity Contributor Assignment Agreement.

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including any modification of or addition to an existing work, that
You submit to the Owner for inclusion in, or documentation of, any of the Projects. "Submit" means any form of
electronic or written communication sent to the Projects' repositories or channels, including pull requests, patches,
issues and comments, excluding communication that You conspicuously mark as "Not a Contribution".

**"Patent Claims"** means any patent claim, owned or controlled by You now or in the future, that would be infringed
by making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or otherwise transferring Your Contribution alone or combined
with the Project to which it was submitted.

## 2. Assignment of rights

2.1 You hereby assign to the Owner, as far as the law allows, all right, title and interest in and to the copyright and
all other economic (exploitation) rights in each Contribution, upon its Submission, worldwide, for the full term of those rights, including
the right to bring action for past, present and future infringement.

2.2 Where an assignment is not possible or not effective under the law that applies, You grant the Owner an exclusive,
perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, no-charge, royalty-free license, with the right to sublicense through multiple tiers
and to transfer, to exercise all those rights.

2.3 **Moral rights.** As far as the law allows, You waive, and agree not to assert, any moral rights You hold in Your
Contribution (such as the rights of attribution and integrity) against the Owner, its licensees and successors. Where
moral rights cannot be waived, You undertake not to exercise them in a way that would prevent the uses this Agreement
permits.

2.4 **License back.** The Owner grants You a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable
license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute and sublicense Your Contribution for any purpose. This license does not
extend to the rest of the Projects, which remain under their own published license.

## 3. Patents

You grant the Owner and every recipient of software distributed by the Owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable license under Your Patent Claims to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale,
import and otherwise transfer Your Contribution and the Project to which it was submitted. You also assign to the Owner,
as far as the law allows, any patent rights that cover Your Contribution itself and that You did not hold before making
it. If any entity institutes patent litigation against You or the Owner alleging that Your Contribution or the Project
infringes a patent, the patent licenses that entity received under this Agreement terminate on the date the litigation
is filed.

## 4. Licensing by the Owner

The Owner may license, relicense or otherwise distribute Your Contribution, and any work that includes it, under any
terms of the Owner's choosing, open source or proprietary, in addition to the license under which a Project is
published at the time of Your Contribution. The Owner may transfer the rights it holds under this Agreement; this
Agreement binds and benefits the Owner's successors and assigns.

## 5. No trademark rights

This Agreement grants no right in the names, trademarks, service marks or logos of the Owner or of the Projects
(including "NeuroBrix", "nbxtensor", "NBXGrammar", "NBX Code" and "Nibux"), and no such right passes to You by Your
Contribution.

## 6. Your representations

6.1 Each Contribution is Your original creation, and You are legally entitled to make this assignment and these grants.
If Your employer or any other party has rights in what You create, You represent that You have their written permission
to make the Contribution under this Agreement, that they have waived those rights, or that they have signed the Entity
Contributor Assignment Agreement with the Owner.

6.2 You will include with Your Contribution complete details of any third-party license or other restriction (including
related patents and trademarks) of which You are aware and which applies to any part of it. Work that is not Your
original creation is submitted separately, conspicuously marked "Submitted on behalf of a third party: [name]", with its
source and license.

6.3 You will notify the Owner of any fact that would make these representations inaccurate.

## 7. Disclaimer

You are not expected to provide support for Your Contributions. Unless required by applicable law or agreed to in
writing, You provide Your Contributions "AS IS", WITHOUT WARRANTIES OR CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND, express or implied,
including any warranties or conditions of TITLE, NON-INFRINGEMENT, MERCHANTABILITY or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. Miscellaneous

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions stay in force, and the
invalid provision is replaced by a valid one that comes closest to its intent. This Agreement applies to every Contribution
You have Submitted to any of the Projects before accepting it, and to every Contribution You Submit after.

## 9. Acceptance

You accept this Agreement electronically, from the GitHub account that submits the Contribution, by posting in the pull
request the sentence:

> I have read the NeuroBrix Contributor Assignment Agreement and I hereby sign it.

The record keeps the account, its numeric identifier, the pull request, the date and the version of this Agreement.
