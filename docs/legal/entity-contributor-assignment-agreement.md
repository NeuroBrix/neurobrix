# NeuroBrix Entity Contributor Assignment Agreement — version 1.0

Modelled on the Project Harmony Entity Contributor Assignment Agreement v1.0 (https://www.harmonyagreements.org),
adapted to the NeuroBrix projects. This Agreement lets a legal entity (the "Entity") authorise contributions by its
Designated Employees to the NeuroBrix projects, and transfers the rights in them to the Owner.

## 1. Definitions

"Owner", "Projects", "Contribution" and "Submit" have the meaning given in the NeuroBrix Individual Contributor
Assignment Agreement version 1.0, which is part of this Agreement; the Owner is the owner of the NeuroBrix project, as
identified in the NOTICE file of the repository at the time of the Contribution, together with its successors and
assigns. "You" means the Entity accepting this Agreement, together with every entity that controls it, is controlled
by it or is under common control with it ("control": direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent of the
voting securities, or the power to direct its management). "Designated Employees" means the persons listed in
Schedule A, as updated by You in writing.

## 2. Assignment, moral rights, license back, patents, licensing by the Owner, no trademark rights

Sections 2 (assignment of rights, including 2.3 moral rights and 2.4 license back), 3 (patents), 4 (licensing by the
Owner), 5 (no trademark rights), 7 (disclaimer) and 8 (miscellaneous) of the Individual Contributor Assignment
Agreement apply to every Contribution Submitted by a Designated Employee, with You in place of the individual: You
assign to the Owner the rights in those Contributions and make the grants and waivers described there.

## 3. Your representations

You represent that You hold the rights in the Contributions of Your Designated Employees (by employment or by
agreement) and are legally entitled to make this assignment and these grants; that each Designated Employee is
authorised to Submit Contributions on Your behalf; that each Contribution is an original creation of Your Designated
Employees or is marked and described as the Individual Agreement's section 6.2 requires; and that You will notify the
Owner when a person ceases to be a Designated Employee or when any of these representations becomes inaccurate.
